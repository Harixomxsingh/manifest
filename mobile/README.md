# Morning Manifestation v1.1.2 (Mobile App)

Executive Morning Dispatch ritual app built for Android & iOS with **React Native & Expo**.

---

## 📱 How to Run on Your Android Smartphone

### 1. Install Expo Go on Android
- Download and open the **Expo Go** app from the Google Play Store on your Android phone.

### 2. Start the Expo Dev Server
Open your terminal and run:
```bash
cd mobile
npx expo start
```

### 3. Scan the QR Code
- Open **Expo Go** on your Android smartphone.
- Tap **"Scan QR code"** and scan the QR code printed in your terminal or browser window.
- The app will load directly on your phone!

---

## ✨ Features Included

1. **Party Poppers & Native Haptics**: Celebratory dual confetti particle cannons explode across your phone screen whenever you tap advancement buttons (*Begin Ritual*, *Anchor Mindset*, *Finished Reading*, *Acknowledge*).
2. **Native Text-To-Speech Audio Dispatch**: Tap the speaker icon or *"Listen to Audio Summary"* to hear the morning briefing spoken aloud through your Android phone's speakers using `expo-speech`.
3. **Automatic GPS Weather**: Real-time hourly daylight horizon, temperatures, and attire advice using `expo-location` and Open-Meteo.
4. **Curated Mindset Reading**: James Clear essays with 1-tap direct links to read full articles.
5. **Dawn Aesthetic**: Warm ivory background (`#FDF9F1`), amber gold accents, tactile cards, and smooth navigation.
